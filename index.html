Robot — Hardware & Process (verbatim)

RedWing Tech strives to answer the following problem statement with its current product: can the company manufacture an efficient and versatile product that can develop a neural network quickly, collect quality information as network input, and classify the information as either true or false, all while adhering to customer and game theme preferences, utilizing a simple design, and expending the least resources and energy?

Hardware Creation Process

As per the company’s yearly process, the Department of Building decided to implement a zero-turn mechanism to provide rotation for the robot. This allows the robot to turn in place, which is crucial for maneuvering in tight spaces. The company achieved the zero-turn mechanism by using two torque motors, two 11.5” wheels, and two PVC sliders. The two torque motors are connected to the 11.5” wheels that are directly controlled by the VEX5 controller. The PVC sliders are created from two identical 1” diameter PVC pipes that are 7.75” long with a 1.75” crimp on one end so that it is flat, and a 2” long and 2” wide PVC elbow on the other end. At the end of the crimp, the pipe is then heated and bent to a 45 degree angle. The PVC sliders are not connected to any motor, but allows for the robot to have the zero-turn mechanism because of the sliders present along the circumference of the wheel. The base and big wheels are constructed out of wood.

Claw / Arm Iterations

Version 1: A small triangular shaped claw with a wide range and motion but it wasn't strong enough. Version 2: A longer version of the previous claw to allow a square shape to hold the connection tiles. Version 3: Rack and pinion to hold the tiles and use the flat sides to pick up Failbook/Tweeter Tower Factoids. Rack & Pinion gives the robot the ability to steadily grab and hold the connection tiles. Attached 4 magnets that can hold the factoids upside down on the claw. Can efficiently always pick up two factoids.

Though not mentioned above, it is also understood that the robot must weigh less than or equal to 24 pounds and be able to maintain dimensions of or under 24” × 24” × 24”.
